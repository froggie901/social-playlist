I wrote this little musing to a friend while traveling around Ireland on my honeymoon with my *husband*.

<Poem>
Well hell—o<br/>
Down the well<br/>
H-E-Double Hockey Sticks<br/>
Lots’o bricks.<br/>
Sticks and stones<br/>
And babbling brooks<br/>
And cozy nooks<br/>
And book books.<br/>
And bells<br/>
And many a show and tell<br/>
The book of Kells, well hell.<br/>
Swells and squalls<br/>
Next to beer halls<br/>
While fiddle and pipes call.<br/>
Mulling vignettes of sunsets<br/>
Kismet.
</Poem>

I took this picture from [Kinard Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aj7bVhwA4h9JeVmA9) which is absolutely beautiful. It was a joy to walk down the exposed beach and hear all the waves crashing. Glossy too
