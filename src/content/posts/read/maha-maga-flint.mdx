Stacey Abrams recently had a [podcast](https://youtu.be/g3NO5j1dRZ4) about the connections between the MAHA and MAGA movements. After listening, I started to think about the [public health crisis in Flint, Michigan where lead was found to be contaminating the drinking water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flint_water_crisis).

I think was my first introduction into what would become the MAHA movement, instantly I started to see all these white Moms on Facebook page starting to think about buying [\$300 Berkey water filters for their homes](https://www.berkeywaterfilter.com/shop/#gravity). At the time, I was living in [Columbia , Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boone_County,_Missouri) where there was zero evidence of water contamination.

As a broke graduate student, my immediate thought was why are these people wasting their precious money on this stupid device?

So now, I looked them up.
