Remember when we used to burn CDs to give to our friends, and wait endlessly for the Lime wire downloads to finish? I’m always a bit surprised when I go back and look at the music I’ve had in my headphones.

This month we had a lovely visit with Colin Self, and got to see a preview of the show at Sh’Bang 2025 which is held at the very cool artist collective, [Lookout Arts Quarry](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dZNuNUqTAXgp6bGc7) in Bellevue, WA

So here goes 10 songs in no particular order. Well except maybe the first one!

<Track n="1" artist="Macy Rodman" title="Stairwell" href="https://macyrodman.bandcamp.com/track/stairwell-2" via="Bandcamp">
A new single from Macy?! Helllll ya. Those first ten seconds set the whole mood for the track. Love a song where I have to look up the meaning of a work, [Haneke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Haneke) *Click. Click. Click.*
</Track>

<Track n="2" artist="Hayden Pedigo" title="Long Pond Lily" href="https://haydenpedigo.bandcamp.com/album/ill-be-waving-as-you-drive-away" via="Bandcamp">
He dropped the delightful *I’ll be waving as your drive away* a few months ago, but I really only recently got the chance to listen to it this month. As much as you can call an acoustic album a banger. This one takes the cake. I’m always just mesmerized by the flowing way that the strings seem to sing under his fingers. I think my favorite track might be “Long Pond Lily”, but they are show some serious chops. He’ll playing in [Seattle on Oct 31 at the Fremont Abbey Arts](https://www.fremontabbey.org).
</Track>

<Track n="3" artist="james k" title="Blinkmoth (July Mix)" href="https://jameskmusic.bandcamp.com/album/friend" via="Bandcamp">
Maybe the 90s are really back, because I could definitely see this song playing in the background of montage of important moments. Plus, I know I’m in good company when I see my buddy Rory posting up about it on instagram. Also playing a show in Seattle this month at [Substation on Oct 15](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/james-k-tickets-1452000756349).
</Track>

<Track n="4" artist="Jon Batiste" title="My Heart (feat. Rita Payés)" href="https://music.apple.com/us/album/my-heart-feat-rita-pay%C3%A9s/1693046146?i=1693046155" via="Apple Music">
As Jon says “Wow, well, I must say that was a treat”. A treat indeed. Rita’s voice pairs magically’s with Jon’s on this track. Don’t sleep on this whole album either. It is definetly going on my top ten of the year. So few albums these days tell a really complete story, and this one does so in spades. Mr. Batiste is a national treasure, the energy and music just seems to pour of out him seemlessly. Runner up is the cover (?) of [“Claire de Lune” featuring Kenny G](https://music.apple.com/us/album/clair-de-lune-feat-kenny-g/1693046146?i=1693046158) who shows all the reasons why he should not be laughed at.
</Track>

<Track n="5" artist="Patricia Wolf" title="Woodland encounters" href="https://patriciawolf.bandcamp.com/album/see-through" via="Bandcamp">
As my friend Ava would say, Period. The tremendous beauty and lushness of this record cannot be missed. Like Hayden’s track, I love a song that unwinds like a story. This track sounds like an early spring morning, where the light bounces off newly green shots covered in dew from the cool temperatures creating a prism of colors, and then birds chittering at each other as the daytime begins. This album was done with [Balmat](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/923861-label-of-the-month-balmat) which I think has just been absolutely killing it. This track is from Patricia’s 2022 release, that I was revisiting prior to listening to the latest [Hrafnamynd](https://music.apple.com/us/album/hrafnamynd/1811425164).
</Track>

<Track n="6" artist="oklou" title="take me by the hand (ft. Bladee)" href="https://music.apple.com/us/album/take-me-by-the-hand/1779808073?i=1779808088" via="Apple Music">
Take me by the hand, when the trouble calls our name. First line, and I was hooked on this track. Thank you. Then we have a Bladee feature? Momma, I’m sold. I love the way the lyrics evolve in this track, and the beat just kicks. Also, I was torn between the original and the [remix](https://music.apple.com/us/album/take-me-by-the-hand-aaron-hibell-remix/1817466845?i=1817466853). The remix just takes it all to a completely different level. I’m not sure what that says about me.
</Track>

<Track n="7" artist="Rihanna" title="Raining Men" href="https://music.apple.com/us/album/raining-men-feat-nicki-minaj/1440895867?i=1440896016" via="Apple Music">
Maybe because I’m just waiting for that next Rihanna album to drop, \*\*eyes\*\* but Rihanna was all over my listening history for this month. I remember when this whole album was on constant rotation for me. Rihanna just so you know, I’m doing good things. So can you do a good thing, drop that new album?
</Track>

<Track n="8" artist="Sylvan Esso" title="Coffee" href="https://music.apple.com/us/album/coffee/1730608950?i=1730609161" via="Apple Music">
Maybe it’s nostalgia because Sylvan Esso was also all over my listening history for this month. I’m also waiting on some new music from them, \*\*eyes\*\*. Hopefully now that they’ve got the record label up and running we will see something. The duo always have a delightful way to combine discordant sounds, but tell a harmonious story.
</Track>

<Track n="9" artist="Transa" title="Feel So Different" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transa_(compilation_album)" via="About the album">
[Transa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transa_(compilation_album)) is a masterwork of storytelling. I came to this track through Sharon Van Etten who pairs with Ezra Furman on this haunting track. For me, it feels like the breakup song I’m having with my country. It’s taught me plenty, but the whole time I’ve never seen all that you have spread before me. Would we expect anything different from a track written by Sinead O’ Connor
</Track>

<Track n="10" artist="Olivia Dean" title="So Easy" href="https://music.apple.com/us/album/so-easy-to-fall-in-love/1817609404?i=1817609507" via="Apple Music">
I first heard Olivia Dean on a Fresh Air interview and I’ve been hooked ever since. Her voice just seems to float perfectly right in the middle of the melody. I’m looking forward to hearing her live!
</Track>
